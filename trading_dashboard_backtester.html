VT Markets No-Code Mobile Connection Guide

Since VT Markets uses standard MT4/MT5 logins without direct APIs, follow these zero-coding options to automate from your smartphone:

Option 1 (Recommended): Telegram Signal Copier EA (100% Mobile)

1. Install a free/cheap Telegram Signal Copier EA (like TelegramFXCopier or MetaCopier) directly into your VT Markets MetaTrader account.
2. Create a private Telegram channel.
3. Enter your Telegram Bot Token in the settings of this dashboard.
4. Whenever this dashboard triggers a trade, it instantly broadcasts `BUY XAUUSD 0.01` to Telegram, and the EA automatically opens the position in VT Markets on your phone!

Option 2: MetaAPI Cloud Bridge (Zero-Coding Cloud)

1. Create a free account on MetaAPI.cloud.
2. Enter your VT Markets MT4/MT5 account number, password, and server (e.g. `VTMarkets-Live`).
3. Paste the generated MetaAPI URL into this dashboard's settings. Trades will trigger directly on VT Markets' servers in real-time.
